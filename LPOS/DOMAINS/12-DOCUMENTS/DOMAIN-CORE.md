# 12-DOCUMENTS

Version: 1.0.0

Canonical location: Artifact registry through PROVENANCE/DATASETS with current native identity/version; originals remain at their source.

Protocol: Read current contents before using a document. Documents can explain facts governed by another domain but do not silently become another mutable operational database.

Current state: retrieve the domain-filtered shared DOMAIN_STATE and the exact native dataset in STORAGE-MAP. Shared logs expose domain-filtered CHANGELOG (OPERATIONS), SOURCE (PROVENANCE) and INCIDENTS, rather than twelve duplicate log files. Empty/inactive domains remain registered routes with no invented records.
